ChillBox Privacy Policy

ChillBox works offline. No account, login, email or phone number is needed to play, and the game has no server of its own.

Data stored on your device: your best scores and settings (sound, vibration). If you add pictures to the Ludo board, they are picked from your own gallery and stay on your device. ChillBox never uploads them.

Ads: ChillBox shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the main menu, a video ad if you choose to continue a run, and a full-screen ad after some games. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use device information such as your Advertising ID, IP address, device type and how you interact with ads. This only happens while your device is online. Learn more: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings, Privacy, Ads. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent and can change your choice later in Settings, Ad and privacy choices.

Children: ChillBox is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Purchases: there are no in-app purchases.

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will appear here.
